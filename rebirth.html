Rebirth guide

Read the live panel before you rebirth

Rebirth is real, but exact rewards and drawbacks can change. Use the game's current explanation as the source of truth.

What is verified about rebirth

The developer's update log confirms a rebirth system and an in-game screen designed to show its benefits and drawbacks. Rebirth-related badges also exist, but a badge is not proof of a current multiplier or reset rule.

Advertisement

Before you confirm

  1. Open the current rebirth panel in your live game session.
  2. Read every listed benefit rather than relying on an older guide.
  3. Read every listed drawback and reset effect.
  4. Decide whether your current cinema milestone is worth giving up for those live benefits.
  5. If the panel differs from a website or video, trust the current game panel.
This page intentionally does not promise a profit multiplier, required cash amount, reset list, or reward total.

Avoid outdated rebirth claims

Do not rebirth because an old page says the first reset has a particular multiplier. The official developer source directs players to the in-game benefits-and-drawbacks screen, so that live panel is the safer decision tool.

Still working on the first theater? Return to the progression roadmap and complete the original cinema before treating rebirth as the next priority.
